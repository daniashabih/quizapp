What is Hangbug?

Hangbug (https://www.hangbug.com/) is an online technology learning and skill assessment platform where students and developers practice technology-related multiple-choice (MCQ) quizzes, test their programming and web development knowledge, explore coding and typing games, track their quiz performance and progress, earn score-based certificates, and share earned certificates on LinkedIn.

Technology Quiz Categories

Practice interactive quizzes across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, jQuery, PHP, Laravel, MySQL, Flutter, Dart, C#, React, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, SQL, SQL Server, Python, Agile and DevOps, Azure, SEO, Power BI, and Excel.
